# THE FRENCH WORLD — Editorial architecture & production plan

A bilingual (French ⇄ Simplified Chinese) reading magazine, A1 → B2 (+ a "Vers le C1" bonus issue),
dark mode, sized for an iPad (A16) in portrait: **160 × 230 mm** pages.

## 5. Grammar reminders (« Rappel ») — what the reading exercises, issue by issue

Each reminder = 2–4 lines + one example from the text + `→ Grammaire, ch. N` (chapter of the grammar book in this repo).

| Issue | Grammar pieces |
|---|---|
| N°1 A1 | être & avoir · nouns, gender, articles · present of -er verbs · negation · questions |
| N°2 A1 | aller/faire/venir/prendre · adjectives (agreement, position) · possessives · partitive & quantities · numbers, time |
| N°3 A1 | pronominal verbs · futur proche · pouvoir/vouloir/devoir · question words · tu/vous & register |
| N°4 A2 | passé composé (avoir; participles) · passé composé (être; agreement) · time markers |
| N°5 A2 | imparfait · COD pronouns · demonstratives · il y a / depuis / pendant |
| N°6 A2 | imparfait vs passé composé · COI pronouns · y & en · comparative & superlative |
| N°7 B1 | futur simple · relative pronouns qui/que/où/dont · plus-que-parfait (intro) · narrative tenses in depth |
| N°8 B1 | conditionnel présent · si + imparfait · pronoun order · imperative with pronouns |
| N°9 B1 | subjonctif présent · opinion: indicatif vs subjonctif · reported speech · cause/consequence/opposition connectors |
| N°10 B2 | plus-que-parfait · conditionnel passé · si + plus-que-parfait · sequence of tenses |
| N°11 B2 | advanced subjunctive (bien que, pour que…, subjonctif passé) · gérondif & participe présent · passive |
| N°12 B2 | register (spoken vs written) · nuanced connectors · mise en relief · lequel/auquel/duquel |
| N°13 C1 | passé simple (reading) · nominalisation · style indirect libre · inversion |

## 6. Source format (`src/*.fw`)

One file per issue part. Directives start with `@`; blocks end with `@end`.

```
@issue 4 | A2 | Hier | 昨天 | tagline FR || tagline ZH
@cast Marie=玛丽, Lucas=卢卡斯
@piece serial | Titre | 中文标题 | A2            (kicker | title | zh title | level)
@deck Accroche en français || 中文导语
@meta characters=…; setting=…; themes=…; topics=travail, logement
@part B1 | Sous-titre | 中文                     (new page, level chip; triptychs)
French sentence. || 中文翻译。                    (aligned, numbered row)
[Marie] Réplique. || 台词。                       (dialogue row with speaker)
@vocab
mot | pos | ipa | 中文 | english | Exemple. | 例句。 | collocations | famille
@end
@expr
expression | 中文 | registre | usage | Exemple. || 例句。
@end
@rappel Titre | 中文 | A2 | 11      (short reminder, ch. = grammar book chapter)
2–4 lines …  `= FR || ZH` example line
@end
@note culture|quebec|slang|think|funny|tip|warn | Title
@compare Title  /  @versions Title  /  @poem Title | author  /  @quote FR || ZH || source
@q Title … @end   @a … @end (→ answer key)   @speak … @end   @md … @end
@badge Nom || 中文 || condition
```

## 8. Visual specification

- Page 160 × 230 mm (iPad portrait ratio), margins 15/12/14/12 mm, background #0f0f0f.
- French #FFFFFF, Source Serif 4, 10.4 pt / 1.48 · Chinese #D0D0D0, Noto Sans SC 9.4 pt / 1.62.
- Titles: Playfair Display; labels: Inter (letter-spaced caps); IPA: Charis SIL.
- Accents: gold #C9A55C (stories, vocab), blue #7BA3D6 (grammar), muted orange #D9895B (slang, warnings, humour).
- Bilingual row = one grid row [nº | FR | ZH], never split across pages.
- Running header: masthead (left) + issue · level · title (right); page number bottom centre.
- Clickable TOC, per-issue contents, PDF bookmarks, vocabulary/grammar/expression indexes link to pages.

## 9. Production status

- **Done:** front matter, Ouverture, N°1–N°13, TCF Canada section, back matter (answer key, vocabulary index, expressions glossary, TCF vocabulary by theme, grammar index, content index).
- Totals: 191 pieces · 2,231 aligned rows · 1,717 vocabulary entries · 283 expressions · 53 grammar reminders · ≈ 710 pages.
- Batch 1 (A1–A2) delivered first; batch 2 (B1, B2, C1, TCF) completed after the reader approved batch 1.
- Grammar is **reminders only** (reader's request): `@rappel Title | 中文 | level | chapter` → chapter of `French_Grammar_TCF_Canada_B2.pdf`.
- Before adding vocabulary: check `build/headwords.txt`. Mark deliberate repeats with `~word` (review card). Duplicate check = headword + part of speech.
- The TCF section is `@issue TCF | TCF | …` (non-numbered issues are supported by the build).
