# Study guide · 学习指南 — The French World

**Goal:** read your way from A1 to B2 (TCF Canada in about six months), with grammar as short reminders only.
The full grammar explanations live in `../French_Grammar_TCF_Canada_B2.pdf`; every « Rappel » box gives the chapter number.

## The six-month plan · 六个月计划

| Weeks · 周 | Read · 阅读 | You can then… · 你将能够 |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | N°1 → N°3 (A1) | introduce yourself, order, ask questions, understand short texts · 自我介绍、点餐、提问、读懂短文 |
| 7–12 | N°4 → N°6 (A2) | tell a story in the past, talk about work, housing, health · 用过去时讲故事，谈工作、住房、健康 |
| 13–18 | N°7 → N°9 (B1) — *batch 2* | give and defend an opinion, imagine, argue · 表达并捍卫观点、假设、论证 |
| 19–24 | N°10 → N°13 (B2 → C1) — *batch 2* | nuance, debate, understand implicit meaning · 表达细微差别、辩论、理解言外之意 |
| 25–26 | TCF Canada section — *batch 2* | mock tests, writing and speaking · 模拟题、写作与口语 |

## One session (30–40 min) · 每次学习

1. **Read** one piece without the Chinese (10 min). 先不看中文读一篇。
2. **Re-read** with the Chinese column and the vocabulary cards (10 min). 对照中文和词汇卡再读一遍。
3. **Cover the French**, rebuild 5 sentences from the Chinese (5 min). 遮住法语，根据中文还原 5 句。
4. **Read 5 sentences aloud** — iPad: Réglages → Accessibilité → Contenu énoncé → Énoncer la sélection (5 min). 大声朗读 5 句（可用 iPad 的“朗读所选项”）。
5. **Evening:** review the day's words in Anki with `anki_vocabulary.tsv` (5 min). 晚上用 Anki 复习。

## Importing the vocabulary into Anki · 导入 Anki

File → Import → `anki_vocabulary.tsv`. The file declares tab separators, HTML and three fields (Front, Back, Tags).
Tags: `FW::A1`, `FW::A2`, and one tag per TCF topic (`FW::travail`, `FW::logement`, `FW::santé`…), so you can study by level or by topic.

## Tracking your progress · 追踪进度

- **Word counter** at the end of every issue (words + expressions met since page 1).
- **Reading log**: tick each piece with your stylus.
- **Badges**: five per issue, each tied to a concrete action (say a sentence to a real person, write a refusal message, record your one-minute presentation…).

## What is not included · 未包含的内容

- No audio files. Every vocabulary entry has IPA, and the iPad can read any selected sentence aloud.
- The PDF has no interactive form fields: tick boxes with a stylus or an annotation app.
