## Pourquoi les pronoms ? · Why pronouns? · 为什么要用代词

French dislikes repeating nouns. Compare:

:::ex
Tu connais **Marie** ? — Oui, je connais **Marie**. J'ai vu **Marie** hier. || (repetitive) || 重复——不自然
Tu connais Marie ? — Oui, je **la** connais. Je **l'**ai vue hier. || Do you know Marie? — Yes, I know her. I saw her yesterday. || 你认识玛丽吗？——认识，我昨天见过她。
:::

## COD : le, la, les · Direct objects · 直接宾语代词

A **direct object** (COD) follows the verb **without a preposition**: *Je vois **Marie**. Je lis **le journal**. J'aime **mes enfants**.*

| Person | COD |
|---|---|
| me / I | **me** (m') |
| you (tu) | **te** (t') |
| him, it (m.) | **le** (l') |
| her, it (f.) | **la** (l') |
| us | **nous** |
| you (vous) | **vous** |
| them | **les** |

:::rule Position: BEFORE the conjugated verb · 放在变位动词前面
*Je **le** vois.* (I see him/it.) · *Je ne **le** vois pas.* · *Je **l'**ai vu.*
English and Chinese put the object **after** the verb ("I see **him**", "我看见**他**") — French puts the pronoun **before**.
:::

:::ex
Le café, je **le** prends sans sucre. || Coffee — I take it without sugar. || 咖啡我不加糖。
Tes clés ? Je **les** ai mises sur la table. || Your keys? I put them on the table. || 你的钥匙？我放桌上了。（les 在前 → mises 配合）
Il **m'**aime, il ne **m'**aime pas… || He loves me, he loves me not… || 他爱我，他不爱我……
Je **vous** remercie. || Thank you (I thank you). || 谢谢您。
:::

:::cn 中文的"把"字句帮你理解
中文"我**把它**放在桌上了"——宾语提前到动词前！法语 *Je **l'**ai mis sur la table* 正好也是"我它放了"。用"把"字句的感觉来记法语代词的位置。
:::

## COI : lui, leur · Indirect objects · 间接宾语代词

An **indirect object** (COI) is a **person** introduced by **à**: *Je parle **à Paul**. J'écris **à mes parents**.*

| Person | COI |
|---|---|
| (to) me | **me** (m') |
| (to) you | **te** (t') |
| (to) him / her | **lui** |
| (to) us | **nous** |
| (to) you | **vous** |
| (to) them | **leur** |

:::ex
Je téléphone **à ma mère**. → Je **lui** téléphone. || I call my mother → I call her. || 我给妈妈打电话 → 我给她打电话。
J'ai écrit **à mes amis**. → Je **leur** ai écrit. || I wrote to my friends → I wrote to them. || 我给朋友们写信了 → 我给他们写了。（COI → 无配合）
Il **me** demande de l'aide. || He asks me for help. || 他向我求助。
Ça **vous** plaît ? || Do you like it? (Does it please you?) || 您喜欢吗？
:::

:::warn lui = him AND her · lui 既是"他"也是"她"
*Je **lui** parle* = I speak to him **or** to her. And ***leur*** (pronoun) never takes an *-s*: *Je **leur** donne **leurs** livres.* (I give them their books — the second *leurs* is a possessive.)
:::

### COD or COI? It depends on the verb · 看动词

The key is whether the French verb uses **à + person**. Some verbs differ from English!

| Verb + **COD** (no *à*) | Verb + **COI** (*à* + person) |
|---|---|
| aider qqn — to help | téléphoner **à** qqn — to phone |
| attendre qqn — to wait for | parler **à** — to speak to |
| chercher qqn — to look for | répondre **à** — to answer |
| écouter qqn — to listen to | demander **à** — to ask |
| regarder qqn — to look at | dire **à** — to tell |
| remercier qqn — to thank | donner / envoyer / offrir / prêter **à** — to give/send/offer/lend |
| inviter qqn — to invite | conseiller **à** — to advise |
| appeler qqn — to call | plaire **à** — to please |
| rencontrer qqn — to meet | ressembler **à** — to look like |
| croire qqn — to believe | sourire **à** — to smile at |

:::ex
J'**aide** ma voisine. → Je **l'**aide. || I help my neighbour → I help her. || 我帮邻居 → 我帮她。（COD）
Je **téléphone à** ma voisine. → Je **lui** téléphone. || I phone my neighbour → I phone her. || 我给邻居打电话 → 给她打。（COI）
Tu **ressembles à** ton père. → Tu **lui** ressembles. || You look like your father → You look like him. || 你像你爸爸 → 你像他。
:::

:::memo Verbs of communication = à · 交流类动词 = à
Verbs of **giving and communicating** (*donner, dire, parler, écrire, envoyer, demander, répondre, téléphoner, expliquer, montrer, prêter, offrir, conseiller, permettre, interdire, promettre*) use ***à* + person** → *lui / leur*. Imagine a gift travelling **à** (to) someone.
:::

## La place des pronoms · Position in every structure · 各种结构中的位置

| Structure | Position | Example |
|---|---|---|
| present, imparfait, futur | before the verb | Je **le** vois. Je **le** verrai. |
| compound tenses | before the auxiliary | Je **l'**ai vu. Je **l'**avais vu. |
| negation | ne + pronoun + verb + pas | Je **ne le** vois **pas**. Je **ne l'**ai **pas** vu. |
| verb + infinitive | before the **infinitive** | Je vais **le** voir. Je veux **lui** parler. Je ne peux pas **le** faire. |
| positive imperative | **after** the verb, with hyphen | Regarde-**le** ! Donne-**lui** ! Aide-**moi** ! |
| negative imperative | before the verb | Ne **le** regarde pas ! Ne **lui** donne rien ! |

:::ex
Ce film ? Je **vais le voir** ce soir. || That film? I'm going to see it tonight. || 那部电影？我今晚去看。
Mes parents ? Je **dois leur écrire**. || My parents? I have to write to them. || 我父母？我得给他们写信。
**Appelle-moi** demain ! — D'accord, je **t'**appelle. || Call me tomorrow! — OK, I'll call you. || 明天给我打电话！——好的。
**Ne me** dérangez **pas**, s'il vous plaît. || Please don't disturb me. || 请不要打扰我。
:::

:::warn Imperative: me → moi, te → toi
In a **positive** imperative, *me / te* become ***moi / toi***: *Dis-**moi** ! Lève-**toi** !* — but in the negative: *Ne **me** dis pas ! Ne **te** lève pas !*
:::

### Agreement reminder · 配合提醒

With ***avoir***, the past participle agrees with a **COD placed before** (*le, la, les, que*), **never** with a COI:

:::ex
Ma voiture ? Je **l'**ai vendu**e**. || My car? I sold it. || 我的车？我卖掉了。
Mes sœurs ? Je **les** ai appelé**es**. || My sisters? I called them. || 我的姐妹？我给她们打过电话了。（appeler + COD → 配合）
Mes sœurs ? Je **leur** ai téléphoné. || My sisters? I phoned them. || 我给她们打了电话。（COI → 不配合）
:::

## Le pronom neutre « le » · The neutral *le* · 中性代词 le (B1–B2)

***le*** can replace a whole **idea, clause or adjective** — English often says nothing or "so / it".

:::ex
Il est parti ? — Oui, je **le** sais. || Has he left? — Yes, I know (it). || 他走了？——是的，我知道。
Tu es fatiguée ? — Oui, je **le** suis. || Are you tired? — Yes, I am. || 你累了吗？——是的。
Il faut réserver ? — Je ne **le** pense pas. || Do we need to book? — I don't think so. || 要预订吗？——我想不用。
Comme je **l'**ai dit, ce projet est important. || As I said, this project is important. || 正如我所说，这个项目很重要。
:::

:::tip
*comme je l'ai dit, comme vous le savez, comme on le voit* — elegant connectors for the **writing** test.
:::

## Les pronoms toniques · Stressed pronouns · 重读代词

| moi | toi | lui | elle | soi | nous | vous | eux | elles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| me | you | him | her | oneself | us | you | them (m.) | them (f.) |

**Uses · 用法:**

:::ex
**Moi**, j'adore l'hiver. **Lui**, il le déteste. || Me, I love winter. Him, he hates it. || 我呢，喜欢冬天；他呢，讨厌冬天。（强调）
Je pars avec **eux**. C'est pour **toi**. Chez **moi**. || I'm leaving with them. It's for you. At my place. || 介词后
C'est **moi** qui ai raison. || I'm the one who's right. || 是我说对了。（c'est … qui）
Qui veut du café ? — **Moi** ! || Who wants coffee? — Me! || 谁要咖啡？——我！（单独使用）
Il est plus grand que **moi**. || He's taller than me. || 他比我高。（比较）
J'ai fait ça **moi-même**. || I did it myself. || 我自己做的。
:::

:::warn penser à, s'intéresser à… + person = à + stressed pronoun
A few verbs with *à* + person **don't** use *lui/leur* but ***à* + stressed pronoun**: *penser **à**, songer **à**, faire attention **à**, s'intéresser **à**, s'adresser **à**, tenir **à**, être **à***, and reflexive verbs.
*Je pense **à elle**.* ✗ *Je lui pense.* · *Fais attention **à lui** !*
For **things**, these verbs use ***y*** (Ch. 16): *J'**y** pense.*
:::
