<span class="level">B1</span>

<div class="goals" markdown="1">
**Objectifs ·** use *y* and *en* correctly (two tiny words that are everywhere in French), and combine two pronouns in the right order. y 和 en 是法语里最"小"却最常见的词；掌握双代词的顺序。
</div>

## Le pronom Y · *there / it* · 代词 y

***y*** replaces:

**1. A place** introduced by *à, en, dans, sur, chez…* — "there" · 代替地点

:::ex
Tu vas **à Toronto** ? — Oui, j'**y** vais demain. || Are you going to Toronto? — Yes, I'm going (there) tomorrow. || 你去多伦多吗？——是的，我明天去。
Elle habite **en Alberta** depuis 2019 ? — Oui, elle **y** habite depuis 2019. || Has she lived in Alberta since 2019? — Yes. || 她从 2019 年起住在阿尔伯塔吗？——是的。
On **y** va ! || Let's go! || 走吧！
:::

**2. *à* + a thing or idea** (not a person) · 代替"à + 事物"

:::ex
Tu penses **à ton examen** ? — Oui, j'**y** pense tout le temps. || Are you thinking about your exam? — Yes, all the time. || 你在想考试吗？——一直在想。
Il s'intéresse **à la politique** ? — Non, il ne s'**y** intéresse pas. || Is he interested in politics? — No, he isn't. || 他对政治感兴趣吗？——不感兴趣。
Vous avez répondu **à son courriel** ? — Oui, j'**y** ai répondu. || Did you answer his email? — Yes, I did. || 您回复他的邮件了吗？——回了。
:::

:::rule à + person → lui/leur · à + thing → y
*Je réponds **au client** → Je **lui** réponds.* · *Je réponds **à la lettre** → J'**y** réponds.*
(Except *penser à, s'intéresser à…* + person → *à lui, à elle*, Ch. 15.)
:::

**Fixed expressions · 固定表达:** *Ça y est !* (That's it! Done!) · *Je n'y peux rien.* (I can't do anything about it.) · *Il y a* (there is) · *J'y suis.* (I get it / I'm there.) · *Vas-y ! Allez-y !* (Go ahead!) · *s'y connaître en* (to know a lot about) · *s'y prendre* (to go about it).

## Le pronom EN · *some, of it, from there* · 代词 en

***en*** replaces:

**1. A noun with a partitive or indefinite article (*du, de la, des, de*)** · 代替部分冠词/不定冠词 + 名词

:::ex
Tu veux **du café** ? — Oui, j'**en** veux bien. || Do you want some coffee? — Yes, I'd like some. || 你要咖啡吗？——好啊，来一点。
Il a **des enfants** ? — Non, il n'**en** a pas. || Does he have children? — No, he doesn't (have any). || 他有孩子吗？——没有。
:::

**2. A noun with a number or quantity — keep the number/quantity at the end!** · 代替数量词后的名词，数量词保留在句尾

:::ex
Combien de frères as-tu ? — J'**en** ai **deux**. || How many brothers do you have? — I have two. || 你有几个兄弟？——两个。
Tu as acheté **des pommes** ? — Oui, j'**en** ai acheté **un kilo**. || Did you buy apples? — Yes, I bought a kilo. || 你买苹果了吗？——买了一公斤。
Il y a **beaucoup de neige** ? — Oui, il y **en** a **beaucoup**. || Is there a lot of snow? — Yes, there's a lot. || 雪多吗？——很多。
Tu as **une voiture** ? — Oui, j'**en** ai **une**. || Do you have a car? — Yes, I have one. || 你有车吗？——有一辆。
:::

:::warn Don't forget *en* with numbers · 数字句子里别忘了 en
English and Chinese simply say "I have two" / "我有两个". French **must** add *en*: ✓ *J'**en** ai deux.* ✗ *J'ai deux.* This is one of the most common mistakes at B1.
:::

**3. *de* + a thing or idea (verbs with *de*)** · 代替"de + 事物"

:::ex
Tu as besoin **de ton ordinateur** ? — Oui, j'**en** ai besoin. || Do you need your computer? — Yes, I need it. || 你需要电脑吗？——需要。
Il parle **de son voyage** ? — Oui, il **en** parle tout le temps. || Does he talk about his trip? — Yes, all the time. || 他谈他的旅行吗？——一直在谈。
Je suis content **de mes résultats**. → J'**en** suis content. || I'm happy with my results → I'm happy with them. || 我对成绩很满意。
:::

**4. A place introduced by *de* — "from there"** · 代替"从某地"

:::ex
Tu reviens **de la banque** ? — Oui, j'**en** reviens. || Are you coming back from the bank? — Yes, I am. || 你从银行回来吗？——是的。
:::

:::rule de + person → de + stressed pronoun
*Je parle **de mon père** → Je parle **de lui**.* (Not *j'en parle* — though you'll hear it.) *en* is for things.
:::

**Fixed expressions · 固定表达:** *Je m'**en** vais.* (I'm leaving.) · *J'**en** ai marre / assez.* (I'm fed up.) · *Je n'**en** peux plus.* (I can't take it anymore.) · *Ne t'**en** fais pas.* (Don't worry.) · *Je t'**en** prie.* (You're welcome / Please.) · *Où **en** es-tu ?* (Where are you at? How far have you got?) · *s'**en** sortir* (to cope, manage) · *en vouloir à qqn* (to hold a grudge).

:::real Everyday French
*Ne t'en fais pas* and *je n'en peux plus* are among the most frequent spoken phrases. In Québec: ***J'en ai plein mon casque !*** (I've had enough!). And *s'en sortir* is perfect for the speaking test: *Au début c'était difficile, mais maintenant je **m'en sors** bien.* (At first it was hard, but now I'm coping well.)
:::

## L'ordre des pronoms doubles · Double pronouns · 双代词顺序

When two pronouns appear together, the order is fixed:

:::rule The order chart · 顺序表
```
  me                                            
  te      le                                     
  se  →   la   →   lui   →   y   →   en   →  VERB
  nous    les      leur                          
  vous                                           
```
**1st/2nd person → le/la/les → lui/leur → y → en**
:::

:::ex
Il **me le** donne. || He gives it to me. || 他把它给我。
Je **te l'**ai dit ! || I told you (so)! || 我跟你说过了！
Je **le lui** ai envoyé. || I sent it to him/her. || 我把它寄给他/她了。
Elle **les leur** a montrées. || She showed them to them. || 她把它们给他们看了。
Il **y en** a trois. || There are three (of them). || 有三个。
Je **vous en** prie. || You're welcome. || 不客气。
**Je ne la lui** ai **pas** prêtée. || I didn't lend it to him. || 我没有把它借给他。
:::

:::memo
**"me-te-se-nous-vous" → "le-la-les" → "lui-leur" → "y" → "en"** — Say it like a chant: "***moi-toi, le-la-les, lui-leur, Y, EN***". For the last two, think of the everyday phrase ***il y en a*** (there are some): **y** always comes before **en**.
:::

### Positive imperative · 肯定命令式

After the verb, the order is: **verb – COD – COI – y – en** (and *moi/toi* become *moi/toi*, but *m'/t'* before *en*).

:::ex
Donne-**le-moi** ! || Give it to me! || 把它给我！
Envoyez-**la-leur**. || Send it to them. || 把它寄给他们。
Donne-**m'en** un peu. || Give me some. || 给我一点。
Vas-**y** ! (with *-s* before *y*) || Go on! || 去吧！加油！
Manges-**en** ! || Eat some! || 吃点吧！
:::

:::tip Real life: avoid "pronoun pile-ups" · 实际使用：避免代词堆积
Native speakers often simplify double pronouns in speech: *Je lui ai donné* (instead of *je le lui ai donné*). For the TCF, mastering ***il y en a, je vous en prie, je te l'ai dit, je le lui ai dit*** is enough to sound natural. In writing, if a double pronoun feels unsure, rephrase with a noun.
:::
