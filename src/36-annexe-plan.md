# Annexe C · Plan d'étude de A1 à B2 <small>A study plan from A1 to B2 · 从 A1 到 B2 的学习计划</small>

Going from A1 to a solid B2 typically takes **600–800 hours** of total study for a motivated adult who speaks English (official estimates vary). With about **2 hours a day**, that's **9–12 months**. This book covers the grammar; you must add listening, reading, speaking and vocabulary every day. 从 A1 到扎实的 B2 通常需要 600–800 小时；每天 2 小时，约 9–12 个月。

:::rule Your daily 2 hours · 每天两小时
| Time | Activity · 活动 |
|---|---|
| 30 min | **Grammar** — one section of this book: read, say examples aloud, back-translate (中→法), exercises |
| 30 min | **Listening** — podcasts, radio, series with French subtitles; 10 min shadowing |
| 20 min | **Vocabulary** — Anki/flashcards; always with article and an example sentence |
| 20 min | **Reading** — news (Radio-Canada, *Le Devoir*, *La Presse*, 1jour1actu for easy news) |
| 20 min | **Output** — write 5–10 sentences or speak 5 minutes (record yourself, or a tutor/language exchange) |
:::

## Phase 1 · A1 → A2 (months 1–3) · 打基础

| Week | Grammar (chapters) | Output goal · 输出目标 |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Intro, Ch. 1–2 (être, avoir, nouns) | Introduce yourself in 10 sentences |
| 3–4 | Ch. 3–4 (articles, adjectives) | Describe your home, your city, a person |
| 5–6 | Ch. 5–6 (present tense, irregular verbs) | Describe your daily routine |
| 7–8 | Ch. 7–8 (negation, questions) | Ask 20 questions about a topic |
| 9–10 | Ch. 9–10 (possessives, prepositions) | Describe your family and your trip to Canada |
| 11–12 | Ch. 11 (passé composé) + review | Tell your last weekend |

## Phase 2 · A2 → B1 (months 4–6) · 叙事与交流

| Week | Grammar (chapters) | Output goal |
|---|---|---|
| 13–14 | Ch. 12 (imparfait, PC vs imparfait) | Tell a childhood memory |
| 15–16 | Ch. 13–14 (future, pluperfect, time words) | Talk about your plans in Canada |
| 17–18 | Ch. 15–16 (COD, COI, y, en) | Rewrite your old texts with pronouns |
| 19–20 | Ch. 17 (relative pronouns) | Write a 120-word blog post (Task 2 style) |
| 21–22 | Ch. 18–19 (comparison, adverbs, indefinites) | Compare life in China and in Canada |
| 23–24 | Ch. 20 (imperative) + review + **first mock TCF** | Give advice to a newcomer (Task 1) |

## Phase 3 · B1 → B2 (months 7–10) · 观点与论证

| Week | Grammar (chapters) | Output goal |
|---|---|---|
| 25–27 | Ch. 21–22 (conditional, *si*) | Speaking Task 2 role-plays; "If I were mayor…" |
| 28–31 | Ch. 23–24 (subjunctive) | Express wishes, obligations, doubts on social topics |
| 32–33 | Ch. 25 (infinitive, gérondif) | Explain how you learned French |
| 34–35 | Ch. 26–27 (passive, reported speech) | Summarise a news article |
| 36–38 | Ch. 28–29 (connectors, register) | Writing Task 3 essays (2 per week) |
| 39–40 | Ch. 30 (50 errors) + **second mock TCF** | Correct your own error log |

## Phase 4 · Exam preparation (months 10–12) · 考前冲刺

- **Every week**: 1 full mock exam (CO + CE timed), 3 writing tasks (one of each type), 3 speaking tasks recorded and replayed.
- Review Ch. 31–33 and your phrase banks; re-read Ch. 30 before each mock.
- Use official sample tests from France Éducation international and reputable TCF Canada prep materials.
- **Last week**: no new grammar. Sleep, review your error log, re-read your best model texts, practise speaking aloud daily.

:::tip Free and useful resources · 免费且有用的资源
- **Listening**: ICI Radio-Canada Première (OHdio app), RFI *Journal en français facile*, TV5Monde *Apprendre le français*, *InnerFrench* podcast, Québec series on Télé-Québec / Tou.tv.
- **Reading**: *1jour1actu* (easy), Radio-Canada.ca, *Le Devoir*, *La Presse*, *Le Monde*.
- **Grammar practice**: TV5Monde exercises, *Le Point du FLE* (exercise portal).
- **Dictionary**: *Le Robert*, *Larousse*, *WordReference* (FR–EN forum is excellent), *Usito* (Québec dictionary), *Antidote* (paid, excellent grammar checker made in Québec).
- **Speaking**: language exchanges (Tandem, HelloTalk), community centres, *francisation* classes (free for immigrants in Québec), conversation groups in public libraries.
:::

:::cn 写给你的话
从 A1 到 B2 是一段长路，但完全可以走完。记住三件事：
1. **每天接触法语**，哪怕只有 20 分钟，也比周末突击 5 小时更有效。
2. **大胆开口、大胆写**——B2 考的是"用法语完成任务"，不是"零错误"。
3. **把错误当朋友**：建立错题本，每周复习一次。你今天犯的错，就是你明天的进步。

**Bon courage, et à bientôt au Canada ! 加油！**
:::
