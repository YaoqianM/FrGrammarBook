| Épreuve | Format | Durée | Score |
|---|---|---|---|

| **Compréhension écrite** (CE) | 39 MCQs, texts from short notices to opinion articles | 60 min | /699 |
| **Expression écrite** (EE) | 3 tasks (see Ch. 32) | 60 min | /20 |
| **Expression orale** (EO) | 3 tasks with an examiner (see Ch. 33) | 12 min | /20 |

|---|---|---|---|---|

:::tip What this means for you · 这对你意味着什么
NCLC 7 in each skill is the common target (e.g., for many Express Entry programs, and French-language points start there). In writing and speaking, **10/20** is roughly the line — that's "B2 with errors that don't block communication". You don't need perfection. You need **range** (varied structures), **coherence** (connectors), and **mostly correct** basics.
:::

## Compréhension orale · Listening · 听力

### What the questions look like · 题型

- **A1–A2 (Q1–10):** short dialogues, announcements: *Où se trouve la personne ? Que veut-elle acheter ? À quelle heure… ?*
- **B1 (Q11–20):** messages, interviews: *Pourquoi l'homme appelle-t-il ? Que propose la femme ?*
- **B2 (Q21–29):** radio reports, debates: *Quel est l'avis de l'invité ? Que dit-il sur… ?*
- **C1–C2 (Q30–39):** fast, abstract, implicit opinions.

Questions are worth **more points** as difficulty rises — B2 questions matter a lot for NCLC 7.

### Grammar that helps you hear · 帮助听懂的语法

| Listen for… · 听什么 | It tells you… · 它告诉你 | Ch. |
|---|---|---|
| ***ne … pas*** often dropped — listen for ***pas, plus, jamais, rien, personne*** | negation | 7 |
| ***-ait*** [ɛ] vs ***a + participle*** | background vs event | 11–12 |
| [r] before ending: *il parlera, il viendra* | future | 13 |
| ***serait, aurait, pourrait*** + *selon…* | unconfirmed info | 21 |
| ***il faut que, pour que, bien que*** | necessity, purpose, concession | 24 |
| ***pourtant, en revanche, par contre, mais*** | the speaker is about to contradict — the answer is often after it | 28 |
| ***y, en, lui, leur*** | who / what is being referred to | 15–16 |
| liaison [z]: *ils‿ont* vs *ils sont* | *have* vs *are* | 1 |
| *il / ils* + liaison: *il‿arrive* vs *ils‿arrivent* [z] | singular vs plural | 5 |

### Strategy · 策略

1. **Read the question and options before the audio starts.** Underline the key word (*pourquoi, où, quand, avis*).
2. **Predict** the vocabulary you'll hear (if the question is about a hotel: *réservation, chambre, arrivée*).
3. **Distractors** use words from the audio in the wrong context. The correct answer is often a **paraphrase** (*Il est en retard* → "*Il n'arrivera pas à l'heure*").
4. **Opinion questions**: listen for *à mon avis, je trouve que, je ne pense pas que, ce qui me dérange, c'est…* — and especially what comes **after *mais / pourtant***.
5. Never leave a blank — there is no penalty for wrong answers (check the current rules).

:::real Training plan · 训练方法
Listen **every day** for 20 minutes: **ICI Radio-Canada Première** (Canadian radio), **RFI Journal en français facile** (slow news, 10 min), **TV5Monde Apprendre**, podcasts *InnerFrench* and *Français Authentique*. Method: listen once without text → listen with transcript → shadow (repeat) one minute aloud.
:::

## Compréhension écrite · Reading · 阅读

### Text types · 文本类型

Notices and signs → emails, ads → short articles → opinion pieces, editorials, literary or scientific extracts.

### Grammar that helps you read · 帮助阅读的语法

- **Pronouns**: find what *il, celui-ci, ce dernier* (the latter), *dont, lequel, en, y* refer to — many questions test exactly that (*« Ce dernier » désigne…*).
- **Connectors**: *cependant, néanmoins, or* (now, yet), *en effet* (indeed — gives proof), *ainsi* (thus), *d'ailleurs* (moreover/besides) show the logic and the author's **real** opinion.
- **Moods**: conditional = hypothesis or unconfirmed; subjunctive after *bien que* = concession.
- **Nominalisation** in headlines (*Hausse des loyers…*) — Ch. 29.
- **Passive & *on***: who actually does the action? *Le projet a été rejeté par les citoyens* → the citizens rejected it.

### Strategy · 策略

1. **Read the question first**, then scan the text for the answer zone.
2. **Time management**: 60 min / 39 questions ≈ 1.5 min each. Easy questions at the start — do them fast (30 s) to save time for long texts at the end.
3. **Watch for absolute words** in options (*toujours, jamais, tous, seulement*) — often wrong.
4. **The author's opinion** is usually in the conclusion or after a concession (*Certes …, mais …* — the opinion is after *mais*).

:::memo The 6 "trap" words in reading · 阅读中的 6 个陷阱词
***or*** (= yet, but — not gold!) · ***en effet*** (= indeed, introduces evidence) · ***d'ailleurs*** (= besides) · ***certes*** (= admittedly — a concession, the opinion comes after) · ***toutefois*** (= however) · ***ainsi*** (= thus / in this way).
:::

:::cn 中国考生的阅读优势与短板
**优势**：词汇量增长快，法语中大量与英语同源的词（*gouvernement, économie, développement*）可以直接猜。
**短板**：代词指代（*ce dernier, celui-ci, dont*）和逻辑连接词（*or, toutefois*）常被忽视，而这恰恰是 B2 题的考点。做题时把每个代词圈出来、画箭头连到它指代的名词。
:::

:::check
**A.** In each sentence, what is the speaker's real opinion? 1. *Certes, le métro est pratique, mais il est souvent en panne.* 2. *Le projet aurait coûté 2 millions.* — Is the cost confirmed? 3. *Il a refusé l'offre ; or, il avait besoin d'argent.* — What does *or* mean here?

**B.** What does the pronoun refer to? 4. *Marie a parlé à sa voisine. Cette dernière était inquiète.* 5. *Le gouvernement a lancé deux programmes, dont l'un vise les jeunes.*
:::

:::answers
**A.** 1. Negative: the metro often breaks down (the opinion follows *mais*). 2. No — conditional = unconfirmed. 3. *or* = "yet / however": he refused although he needed money.
**B.** 4. *cette dernière* = la voisine (the last-mentioned). 5. *l'un* = one of the two programmes.
:::
