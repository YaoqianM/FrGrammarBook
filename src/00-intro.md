## Ce livre est fait pour toi · Who this book is for · 这本书写给谁

You are at **A1** today. Your goal is **B2** on the **TCF Canada** (usually NCLC 7 or more in each skill). You speak **Chinese** natively and know some **English grammar**. This book uses all three languages on purpose:

- **Français** — the target. Every rule is shown in real French sentences.
- **English** — your "bridge": French and English share ~30% of their vocabulary and many grammar ideas (tenses, relative clauses, passive voice…).
- **中文** — your "safety net": when an idea is new (gender, articles, subjunctive), a Chinese explanation makes it click faster.

## Comment étudier avec ce livre · How to study · 怎么用这本书学习

**1. One chapter = three sessions.**

- *Session 1 — Understand (理解):* read the chapter, say every French example **out loud**.
- *Session 2 — Remember (记住):* the next day, cover the French line and translate the Chinese line back into French. This is the most powerful exercise in the book: **回译法 (back-translation)**.
- *Session 3 — Use (会用):* do the *Exercices*, check the answer key at the end, then write **5 sentences about your own life** with the new structure.

**2. Spaced repetition (间隔重复).** Review each chapter after 1 day, 3 days, 7 days and 21 days. Put the examples you get wrong into Anki or a notebook.

**3. Learn sentences, not words.** Don't memorise *prendre = to take*. Memorise *Je prends le bus tous les matins.* A sentence gives you the gender, the article, the preposition and the conjugation at once.

**4. Speak and write from week one.** Grammar you never use disappears. Every chapter ends with a *real life* section so you can use the structure immediately.

:::cn 中文母语者的三大优势和三大难点
**优势：** ① 你习惯了记忆大量字词，法语单词量对你不是问题；② 中文语序 SVO 和法语基本一致；③ 你学过英语，已经理解"时态""从句""被动"这些概念。

**难点：** ① **阴阳性和冠词**——中文没有，必须"名词+冠词"一起记；② **动词变位**——中文动词不变，法语一个动词有几十种形式，要靠规律而不是死记；③ **时态的"体"**——中文用"了、过、着"，法语用复合过去时/未完成过去时，逻辑不同，本书会专门对比。
:::

## Le français en 10 idées · French in 10 big ideas · 法语十大核心概念

Before the details, here is the whole picture. Everything in this book is an expansion of these ten ideas.

1. **Every noun is masculine or feminine** — *le* livre, *la* table. 每个名词都有阴阳性。
2. **Nouns almost always need an article** — *J'aime **le** café. Je bois **du** café.* 名词前几乎总要冠词。
3. **Adjectives agree** with the noun and usually come **after** it — *une voiture rouge*. 形容词要配合，通常放在名词后。
4. **Verbs change with the person** (*je parle, nous parlons*) and the **tense/mood**. 动词按人称和时态变位。
5. **Two past tenses work together**: *passé composé* = events; *imparfait* = background. 两个过去时分工合作。
6. **Pronouns go before the verb** — *Je **le** vois. Je **lui** parle. J'**y** vais.* 宾语代词放在动词前面。
7. **Negation wraps around the verb** — *Je **ne** parle **pas**.* 否定把动词"夹"起来。
8. **Moods express attitude**: indicative (facts), conditional (imagination/politeness), subjunctive (wishes, emotions, doubt). 语式表达说话人的态度。
9. **French hates hiatus** (two vowels clashing), so it elides and links: *l'ami, j'aime, les‿amis*. 法语讨厌元音相撞：省音与联诵。
10. **Register matters**: spoken French drops *ne*, uses *on* for *nous*, and asks questions with intonation. Written exam French is more formal. 口语和书面语差别很大。

## Les sons et l'orthographe en bref · Sounds & spelling in brief · 发音与拼写速览

This is a grammar book, but you cannot do grammar without hearing it: many grammar endings are **written but not pronounced**.

### Accents · 音符

| Accent | Example | Effect · 作用 |
|---|---|---|
| é (aigu) | café, été | closed [e], like "ay" without the y-glide. 闭口 e |
| è (grave) | mère, très | open [ɛ], like "e" in *bed*. 开口 e |
| ê (circonflexe) | fête, forêt | usually [ɛ]; often marks a lost *s* (fête ↔ *feast*, forêt ↔ *forest*). 常表示古法语丢失的 s |
| à, ù | à, où | no sound change; distinguishes words (*a* has / *à* to; *ou* or / *où* where). 只区分词义 |
| ç (cédille) | ça, garçon | makes c = [s] before a, o, u. 让 c 读 [s] |
| ë, ï (tréma) | Noël, naïf | pronounce the vowels separately. 两个元音分开读 |

:::memo The circumflex trick · 长音符小窍门
When you see **ê, â, î, ô, û**, try adding an **s**: *hôpital → hospital, île → isle, château → castle, forêt → forest, intérêt → interest.* Instant vocabulary from English!
:::

### Silent letters · 不发音的字母

As a rule, **final consonants are silent** — except often **C, R, F, L** (think of the English word **CaReFuL**): *sac, finir, neuf, sel* are pronounced; *petit, grand, vous, nez* are not. The final **-e** is silent: *table* [tabl]. And the verb ending **-ent** (*ils parlent*) is **always** silent.

:::cn 听力提示
法语中很多语法词尾**看得见、听不见**：*il parle / ils parlent* 发音完全一样；*ami / amie* 发音也一样。所以**听力**时要靠冠词和代词判断单复数（*le / les*, *il / ils + 联诵*），**写作**时要特别检查词尾。
:::

### Elision and liaison · 省音与联诵

**Elision (省音):** *le, la, je, me, te, se, ne, de, que, si (before il/ils)* drop their vowel before a vowel or mute *h*:

:::ex
**l'**homme, **l'**école, **j'**aime, **je n'**ai pas, **qu'**il vienne, **s'**il pleut || the man, the school, I love, I don't have, that he come, if it rains || 省音后用撇号连接
:::

**Liaison (联诵):** a normally silent final consonant is pronounced when the next word starts with a vowel: *les‿amis* [lezami], *vous‿avez* [vuzave], *un‿enfant* [œ̃nɑ̃fɑ̃]. Liaison is compulsory after articles and pronouns, forbidden after *et* (*et / un*) and before an aspirated *h* (*les / héros*).

## Le TCF Canada en une page · The exam on one page · 一页看懂 TCF Canada

| Épreuve · Test | Format | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Compréhension orale (listening) | 39 multiple-choice questions, increasing difficulty A1→C2 | 35 min |
| Compréhension écrite (reading) | 39 multiple-choice questions, increasing difficulty | 60 min |
| Expression écrite (writing) | 3 tasks: short message · article/story · argued comparison of two opinions | 60 min |
| Expression orale (speaking) | 3 tasks: guided interview · role-play where *you* ask questions · giving an opinion | 12 min |

Results are converted into **NCLC** levels (Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadiens). B2 corresponds roughly to **NCLC 7–8**; for writing and speaking, NCLC 7 is generally around **10–11 / 20**. Score tables and requirements change — always check the latest official tables of France Éducation international and IRCC for your immigration program.

Part 6 of this book (Ch. 31–33) gives you the full strategy, templates and model answers.

:::tip How grammar earns points in each skill · 语法怎样帮你拿分
- **Reading & listening:** recognising tenses, pronouns (*en, y, lui, dont*) and connectors (*pourtant, en revanche, donc*) tells you *who did what* and *what the author really thinks*.
- **Writing & speaking:** the grid explicitly rewards *étendue* (range) and *correction* (accuracy) of grammar. A B2 candidate uses the subjunctive, the conditional, relative pronouns and logical connectors **naturally and mostly correctly**.
:::
