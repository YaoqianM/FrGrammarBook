# Study guide · 学习指南 — The Spanish World

**Goal:** read your way from A1 to B2 (DELE B2 or SIELE in about six months), with grammar as short reminders only.

## The six-month plan · 六个月计划

| Weeks · 周 | Read · 阅读 | You can then… · 你将能够 |
|---|---|---|
| 1–6 | N.º 1 → N.º 3 (A1) | introduce yourself, order, ask questions · 自我介绍、点餐、提问 |
| 7–12 | N.º 4 → N.º 6 (A2) | tell a story in the past, talk about work and housing · 用过去时讲故事，谈工作和住房 |
| 13–18 | N.º 7 → N.º 9 (B1) | give and defend an opinion, imagine · 表达并捍卫观点、假设 |
| 19–24 | N.º 10 → N.º 13 (B2 → C1) | nuance, debate, read literature · 表达细微差别、辩论、读文学作品 |
| 25–26 | DELE · SIELE section | mock tests, writing and speaking · 模拟题、写作与口语 |

## One session (30–40 min) · 每次学习

1. **Read** one piece without the Chinese (10 min). 先不看中文读一篇。
2. **Re-read** with the Chinese and the vocabulary cards (10 min). 对照中文和词汇卡再读。
3. **Cover the Spanish**, rebuild 5 sentences from the Chinese (5 min). 遮住西语，根据中文还原 5 句。
4. **Read 5 sentences aloud** — iPad: Ajustes → Accesibilidad → Contenido leído → Leer selección; choose a Mexican or Spanish voice. 大声朗读 5 句。
5. **Evening:** review the day's words in Anki with `anki_vocabulary.tsv` (5 min). 晚上用 Anki 复习。

## Anki

File → Import → `anki_vocabulary.tsv` (tab-separated, HTML, fields Front / Back / Tags). Tags: `FW::A1` … `FW::C1` and one per topic (`FW::trabajo`, `FW::vivienda`, `FW::salud`…).

## Mexico or Spain? · 墨西哥还是西班牙？

The text uses Mexican Spanish; boxes marked **⚑ EN ESPAÑA** show what changes in Madrid. For the DELE and SIELE, any standard variety is accepted: pick one and be consistent.
