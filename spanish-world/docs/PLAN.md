# THE SPANISH WORLD — editorial plan

Spanish edition of *The French World* (`../french-world/`), made for a Chinese-speaking friend learning Spanish.
Same structure, same layout, same serial plot and the same Chinese column; Spanish replaces French and
everything French-specific is replaced by its Spanish-world equivalent.

## 1. Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Variety | **Latin American (Mexican) Spanish as the base**; notes `⚑ EN ESPAÑA` for Spain usage (vosotros, *coche*, *vale*, θ…); occasional `⚑ EN OTROS PAÍSES` notes (Argentina voseo, Colombia…) |
| Settings | Paris → **Ciudad de México** (Roma, Condesa, Coyoacán, Centro); Montréal → **Madrid** (Lavapiés, Malasaña, Chamberí) |
| Plot | unchanged from the French serial; names and places adapted |
| French-specific content | replaced by Spanish equivalents: Spanish jokes & slang, Mexico ⇄ Spain dictionary, public-domain Spanish poems, Spanish grammar reminders, **DELE B2 / SIELE** exam section |
| Chinese | Simplified, same style; names and places changed |
| IPA | phonemic, Latin American (seseo, yeísmo): /ˈka.sa/, /ʝ/, /ɲ/, /ɾ/ vs /r/, /x/, /tʃ/; stress mark ˈ always written |
| Grammar | « Recuerda » boxes only (2–4 lines + example); no chapter references (there is no Spanish grammar book in the repo) |
| Delivery | everything at once |

## 6. Spanish-world replacements (selection)

- Exam: TCF Canada → **DELE B2** (Instituto Cervantes) + **SIELE** (always "check the official site").
- Paperwork: Mexico (INE, CURP, RFC, CLABE, comprobante de domicilio, aval / póliza jurídica, IMSS, SAT) and
  Spain (NIE, TIE, empadronamiento, cita previa, fianza, Seguridad Social, Agencia Tributaria, médico de cabecera).
- Humour: Pepito (MX) / Jaimito (ES) jokes, puns, *albur* (with warning), sarcasm; greeting kiss (MX one, ES two).
- Slang: MX güey, chido, neta, padre, chamba, ¿qué onda?, no manches · ES tío, guay, mola, flipar, currar, pasta;
  swearing guide (MX chingar family, pendejo, cabrón · ES joder, coño, hostia, gilipollas; the *coger* trap).
- Poems (public domain): Machado, Bécquer, Sor Juana, Garcilaso, Rubén Darío, Lorca (short excerpt), Cervantes
  (C1 reading); original poems in Spanish. Modern authors only as short attributed quotes.
- Films: *Relatos salvajes* (2014), *Campeones* (2018), *Roma* (2018) replace *La Haine*, *Intouchables*, *Amélie*.
- Language-policy debate: Quebec's Bill 101 → Indigenous languages in Mexico (Ley General de Derechos
  Lingüísticos de los Pueblos Indígenas, 2003).

## 7. Source format

Identical to the French edition (`../french-world/docs/PLAN.md` §6). `@meta topics=` uses the Spanish topic
list: trabajo, vivienda, migración, salud, dinero, economía, sociedad, medio ambiente, tecnología, educación,
transporte, justicia, administración, medios, cultura, relaciones, psicología, consumo, ciencia.
The exam section is `@issue DELE | DELE | …`. Part-of-speech labels: `s. m.`, `s. f.`, `v.`, `v. prnl.`, `adj.`,
`adv.`, `loc.`, `interj.`, `prep.`, `conj.`, `pron.`.
