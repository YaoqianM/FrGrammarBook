# The interview (≈20 questions)

Ask with the multiple-choice question tool, 4 questions per round, recommended option first. Record every answer in `docs/PLAN.md` §1 as a table *Topic | Decision*. The answers the user gave for the French edition are shown as the default.

| # | Topic | Question | Default (French edition) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reader level | Where is the reader today? | "some A1" |
| 2 | Goal & deadline | Exam? When? Why the language? | TCF Canada within 6 months; immigrate, work, social life & dating |
| 3 | Interests | Which themes keep them reading? | dating/flirting, humour, talk shows, debate & persuasion, quotable lines, ideas that challenge how to live, tech/AI, money & business, psychology & philosophy |
| 4 | Feel | Textbook, magazine, or "TikTok"? | "like TikTok": many short varied pieces, visible progress |
| 5 | Variety of the language | Which country is the base? Notes for others? | France base + Québec notes (Spanish: Mexico base + Spain notes) |
| 6 | Native-language column | Script / variety? | Simplified Chinese, full sentence-by-sentence translation |
| 7 | English | Use English glosses? | selective: every vocab card + notes on false friends, idioms, grammar |
| 8 | Slang & swearing | Include? | yes, each with register and when NOT to use it |
| 9 | Romance | How adult? | realistic adult (attraction, jealousy, cheating, breakups, sex implied not graphic); characters may behave badly and grow; how-to pages never teach manipulation |
| 10 | Quotes & literature | Real quotes? Poems? | short real quotes with credit; public-domain poems in full; original poems |
| 11 | Vocabulary density | Light or heavy? | heavy: 10–15 per piece, IPA on every entry |
| 12 | Grammar | Full lessons or reminders? | **short reminders only**, pointing to the user's own grammar book (changed by the user mid-project) |
| 13 | Exam section | Separate? Which exam? | one separate exam section at the end |
| 14 | Progress | How to see progress? | word counter + reading log + badges at the end of each issue |
| 15 | Device | Main reading device? | iPad (A16) portrait; desktop and phone secondary |
| 16 | Theme | Dark / light / both? | dark only |
| 17 | Packaging | One PDF or several? | one complete PDF + spreadsheets, Anki deck, indexes |
| 18 | Delivery | All at once or in batches? | batch 1 = A1+A2, batch 2 = B1+B2+C1+exam (Spanish edition: all at once) |
| 19 | Settings & cast | Which cities? A recurring couple? | Paris → Montréal; Marie & Lucas + flatmate, comic friend, sister abroad, colleague learning the language, grandmother with a secret, the ex, the boss |
| 20 | Final level | How far? | B2, with a C1 bonus issue |

For an **adaptation** of an existing edition ("same book for my friend learning X"), ask only:

1. Which variety is the base, and which variant gets notes?
2. Keep the original cities, or move the story to the new language's world (renaming characters, same plot)?
3. Culture-specific content (jokes, slang, poems, exam): keep and translate, or replace with real equivalents?
4. Deliver all at once or in batches?
5. (If unclear) Same native-language column? Same exam goal?
