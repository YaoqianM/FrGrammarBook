# Adapting an edition: the French → Spanish example

User's instructions for the Spanish edition: "replace French with Spanish, the rest almost unchanged", then chose: Latin American (Mexican) base + notes for Spain; move the story to Spanish-speaking cities with renamed characters and the same plot; replace French-specific content with real Spanish equivalents; deliver all at once. The full decision record is `spanish-world/docs/PLAN.md`.

## What stays

Issue list and levels · serial plot beats and episode titles (translated) · section kinds · piece order inside each issue · the Chinese column (adapt names and places only) · philosophy (Montaigne, Socrates, Epicurus, Epictetus, Sartre, Beauvoir, Pascal, Voltaire, Rawls, Camus — quotes as "traducción libre") · psychology and science pieces with their sources · layout, pipeline, deliverables.

## Checklist for a new language

1. Base variety and the variant(s) that get notes; a matching note kind and register keywords.
2. Two cities (home and destination) with real neighbourhoods, transport, food, weather, paperwork.
3. Cast renamed naturally; keep roles and arcs.
4. For each culture-specific piece, find the real equivalent (jokes the target culture actually tells, slang actually used, a public-domain poet everyone studies at school, a well-known film with a theme that fits the issue).
5. Grammar reminders rewritten for the target language's real difficulties for a Chinese speaker; drop points that do not exist.
6. Exam section rebuilt for the official exam of that language, with "check the official site".
7. Topic list (`TCF_TOPICS`) translated; `@meta topics=` uses it.
