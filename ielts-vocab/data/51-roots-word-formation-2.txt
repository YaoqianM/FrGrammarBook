@unit 51
@title Word Formation II: Latin & Greek Roots
@zh 构词法（二）：拉丁与希腊词根
@level C1–C2 · 高分精修
@intro 学术英语中大约一半以上的词汇来自拉丁语和希腊语（经由法语等语言进入英语）。认识二三十个高频词根，就能在阅读中**猜出大量生词的大致意思**。本单元每组以一个词根开头，列出前面各单元**尚未收录**的代表词；已学过的同根词会在规律框中提到，帮助你把整套书的词汇串起来。

@box tip 词根的正确用法
词根帮助你**猜测和记忆**，但不能代替查词典：很多词的意思已经和词根有了距离（*nice* 来自拉丁语“无知的”）。建议的做法：读到生词 → 用词根猜一个大致意思 → 用上下文验证 → 查词典确认。
@end

## 1 · Seeing, hearing & speaking · 看、听、说

@box rule spect / vis, vid（看） · aud（听） · dict（说）
**spect：** *perspective*（U39）、*spectator*（U8）、*in retrospect*（U35）、*inspect, prospect, spectacle*
**vis / vid：** *visible*、*evident*（U50）、*revise*（U7）、*vision, visual*
**aud：** *audience*（U8）、*audible, audit*
**dict：** *predict*（U13）、*contradictory*（U46）、*dictate, verdict*
@end

- inspect | v. | 检查；视察 | inspect a building/a school | Officials inspect restaurants to make sure they are clean. | W
  fam: inspection (n.) · inspector (n.)
- prospect | n. | 前景；可能性；希望 | job prospects; the prospect of sth | Graduates face good job prospects in engineering. // The prospect of moving abroad excited her. | W
  fam: prospective (adj., 未来的；可能的)
- spectacle | n. | 壮观景象；场面；（复数）眼镜 | — | The fireworks were an amazing spectacle.
  fam: spectacular (adj.)
- introspection | n. | 内省；反省 | — | Writing a diary encourages introspection. | W
- vision | n. | 视力；远见；愿景 | a clear vision for the future | The company has a clear vision for the future. | W
- visual | adj. | 视觉的 | visual aids/learners | Some students are visual learners. | W
  fam: visualise (v.)
- audible | adj. | 听得见的 | barely audible | His voice was barely audible. | W
  fam: inaudible (adj.)
- audit | n. / v. | 审计；审查 | an energy audit | The company carries out an annual audit. | W
- dictate | v. | 口述；命令；支配；决定 | dictate a letter; dictate terms | Fashion trends are often dictated by celebrities. | W
  fam: dictation (n.) · dictator (n.)
- verdict | n. | （陪审团的）裁决；判断；结论 | reach a verdict | The jury returned a verdict of not guilty. | W
  note: *ver-*（真实）+ *dict*（说）= “说出真相”。同根：*verify*（U38）。

## 4 · Going & following · 行进与跟随

## 9 · Other high-value roots · 其他高频词根

- capture | v. / n. | 捕获；夺取；记录（图像、情感） | capture attention/the moment | The photo captures the beauty of the city. // The police captured the thief. | W
  note: *cap / cept / ceive*（拿、取）：*capture, capacity*（U39）、*concept*（U39）、*perceive*（U38）、*exception, intercept*。
- exception | n. | 例外 | with the exception of; make an exception | With the exception of maths, all her grades improved. | W
  fam: exceptional (adj., 杰出的) · exceptionally (adv.)
- autocracy | n. | 独裁统治 | — | Power in an autocracy is held by a single person. | W
  note: *-cracy*（统治）：*democracy*（人民统治，U34）、*bureaucracy*（官僚统治，U34）、*autocracy*（独裁）。
- cyclical | adj. | 周期性的；循环的 | cyclical changes | The economy goes through cyclical ups and downs. | W
  note: *cycl*（圆、循环）：*recycle*（U29）、*bicycle*、*cyclone*（气旋，U10 台风辨析）。
- incision | n. | 切口 | — | The surgeon made a small incision. | W
  note: *cis / cide*（切、杀）：*precise*（U46）、*decisive*（U36）、*pesticide*（U32）、*incision*。
- macroeconomics | n. | 宏观经济学 | — | Macroeconomics studies the economy as a whole. | W
  note: *macro-*（大）↔ *micro-*（小）：*microscope*（U27）、*microeconomics*（研究个人和企业的经济学）。
- telecommunications | n. | 电信 | the telecommunications industry | Shenzhen is home to major telecommunications companies. | W
  note: *tele-*（远）：*telescope, telephone, television, telecommuting*（远程办公）。

@box ielts 词根帮你做阅读
阅读遇到 *The species is **terrestrial**, unlike its **aquatic** relatives.* 即使没见过 *terrestrial*，从 *terr*（土地）和后面的对比 *aquatic*（水生的）也能推出：“陆生的”。这就是词根 + 上下文的威力。
@end
