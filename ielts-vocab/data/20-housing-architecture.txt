## 1 · Renting & owning · 租房与买房

- rent | n. / v. | 租金；租用；出租 | pay the rent; rent a flat | Rent takes up half of his salary. // We rent a two-bedroom flat.
  fam: rental (adj./n.)
- landlord | n. | 房东 | — | Our landlord raised the rent again.
- tenant | n. | 租户；房客 | — | The tenants complained about the broken heating.
- mortgage | n. | 抵押贷款；房贷 | take out a mortgage; pay off a mortgage | They took out a 30-year mortgage to buy the flat.
  ipa: /ˈmɔːɡɪdʒ/
  note: t 不发音 /ˈmɔːɡɪdʒ/。房贷是讨论住房负担时的核心词：*mortgage repayments*（月供）。
- property | n. | 房产；财产；（物质的）特性 | property prices; private property | Property prices in Shenzhen are among the highest in China. // Copper has useful electrical properties.
  note: 学术义 *properties* = 特性（the properties of a material），在科学类阅读中常见。
- real estate | n. | 房地产（美式常用） | the real estate market | He works in real estate.
- housing | n. | 住房（总称，不可数） | affordable housing; housing shortage | The city needs more affordable housing. | W
- housing shortage | n. | 住房短缺 | — | Rapid population growth has led to a housing shortage. | W
- homeowner | n. | 房主 | — | Most homeowners have a mortgage.
- first-time buyer | n. | 首次购房者 | — | High prices make it hard for first-time buyers.
- deposit | n. / v. | 押金；首付；存款；沉积 | pay a deposit; a mineral deposit | You need a 30% deposit to buy a flat.
- move house | phr. | 搬家（英） | — | We moved house three times when I was young. | S
- house-sharing | n. | 合租 | — | House-sharing is common among young workers.
- homeless | adj. / n. | 无家可归的；无家可归者（the homeless） | — | The charity provides food for the homeless.
  fam: homelessness (n.)
- eviction | n. | 驱逐（租户） | face eviction | Some tenants face eviction if they cannot pay the rent. | W
  fam: evict (v.)
- soar | v. | （价格）飙升；高飞 | — | House prices have soared over the past decade.
  note: *soar*、*rocket*、*skyrocket* 都表示“猛涨”，在图表写作中也很有用（第四册 Unit 41）。
- unaffordable | adj. | 负担不起的 | — | Housing has become unaffordable for many young people. | W

## 2 · Types of home · 住房类型

- detached house | n. | 独栋房屋 | — | They live in a large detached house with a garden.
- semi-detached house | n. | 半独立式住宅（两户共用一面墙） | — | Most houses on our street are semi-detached.
- terraced house | n. | 连排房屋（英） | — | Many terraced houses in Britain were built in the 19th century.
- apartment block | n. | 公寓楼（英式也说 block of flats） | — | We live on the 25th floor of an apartment block.
- villa | n. | 别墅 | — | They rented a villa by the sea.
- cottage | n. | 乡村小屋 | — | They have a small cottage in the countryside.
- studio flat | n. | 单间公寓 | — | A studio flat is ideal for one person.
- dormitory | n. | 宿舍（常简称 dorm） | — | First-year students live in dormitories.
- urban village | n. | 城中村 | — | Many migrant workers in Shenzhen live in urban villages.
  note: 深圳的“城中村”（如白石洲、大芬）是城市研究的著名案例，英文文献中常译为 *urban village* 或 *village in the city*。
- gated community | n. | 封闭式小区 | — | Most new housing in Chinese cities is built as gated communities.

## 3 · Buildings & design · 建筑与设计

* architecture | n. | 建筑学；建筑风格 | modern architecture; traditional architecture | The city is known for its modern architecture.
  ipa: /ˈɑːkɪtektʃə/
  fam: architect (n.) /ˈɑːkɪtekt/ · architectural (adj.)
  note: *ch* 读 /k/：*architect, character, chemistry, mechanism, technique*（/tekˈniːk/）。这类词多来自希腊语。
- design | n. / v. | 设计；图案 | building design; be designed to do sth | The building was designed by a famous architect.
  fam: designer (n.)
- construction | n. | 建设；建造；结构 | under construction; the construction industry | A new metro line is under construction.
  fam: construct (v.)
- skyscraper | n. | 摩天大楼 | — | Shenzhen has hundreds of skyscrapers.
  note: 根据世界高层建筑与都市人居学会（CTBUH）的数据，深圳是全球 150 米以上高楼最多的城市之一。具体排名每年都在变化，写作中不需要给出精确数字。
- storey | n. | 楼层（美式 story） | a 30-storey building | They live in a ten-storey building.
- facade | n. | （建筑的）正面；表象 | a glass facade | The building has a striking glass facade. | W
  ipa: /fəˈsɑːd/
- interior | n. / adj. | 内部；室内的 | interior design | The interior of the temple is beautifully decorated.
- exterior | n. / adj. | 外部；外观 | — | The exterior of the house needs painting.
- layout | n. | 布局；版面设计 | the layout of a flat | I like the layout of our new flat — the kitchen is next to the living room.
- functional | adj. | 实用的；功能性的 | — | The building is functional rather than beautiful.
  fam: function (n./v.)
- aesthetic | adj. / n. | 美学的；审美的 | aesthetic value | Some people care more about the aesthetic value of buildings than their function. | W
  ipa: /iːsˈθetɪk/
  fam: aesthetics (n.)
- striking | adj. | 引人注目的；显著的 | a striking design; a striking contrast | The new museum has a striking design.
- eyesore | n. | 难看的东西；碍眼物 | — | Many people think the old factory is an eyesore. | S
- heritage | n. | 遗产；传统 | cultural heritage; heritage sites | Old buildings are part of our cultural heritage. | W
- preserve | v. | 保护；保存；维持 | preserve historic buildings | The government should preserve historic buildings.
  fam: preservation (n.)
- demolish | v. | 拆除；拆毁 | demolish old buildings | Many old houses were demolished to build new roads.
  fam: demolition (n.)
- renovate | v. | 翻新；修复 | renovate a house | They renovated the old warehouse and turned it into a café.
  fam: renovation (n.)
- restore | v. | 修复；恢复 | restore an old temple; restore order | The temple was carefully restored.
  fam: restoration (n.)
  note: *renovate* 侧重“翻新、改造使其更现代”；*restore* 侧重“恢复原貌”（常用于古迹）；*repair* 是一般的“修理”。
- sustainable design | n. | 可持续设计 | — | Sustainable design reduces a building's energy use. | W
- insulation | n. | 隔热（材料）；隔音 | — | Good insulation keeps homes warm in winter.
  fam: insulate (v.)
- ventilation | n. | 通风 | good ventilation | Good ventilation is important in hot, humid cities.
- natural light | n. | 自然光 | — | The room gets a lot of natural light.

@cmp house · home · housing
house | 一栋房子（建筑物） | They live in a big house.
home | 家（情感上的居所，不限于房子） | Home is where the heart is.
housing | 住房（总称，常用于政策讨论） | The government is building more public housing.
@end

@box ielts 写作：现代建筑 vs. 传统建筑
支持保护传统建筑：*preserve cultural heritage*、*attract tourists*、*give a city its unique character*。支持现代建筑：*make better use of limited land*、*meet the housing needs of a growing population*、*energy-efficient design*。高分作文通常会提出平衡方案：*integrate modern design with traditional features*。
@end
