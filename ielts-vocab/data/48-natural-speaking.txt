@unit 48
@title Natural Spoken English for the Speaking Test
@zh 自然口语表达（口语考试专用）
@level B2–C1 · 高分精修
@intro 口语考试的词汇分数不靠“书面大词”，而靠**自然、准确、有变化**的表达：恰当的口语习语、短语动词，以及给自己思考时间的“缓冲语”。这一单元的所有表达都适合在口语中使用，但其中大部分**不适合**写进 Task 2 作文。请注意：习语用错比不用更糟，只挑你真正理解、能自然说出口的几个。

@box warn 口语习语的使用原则
1. **少而准**：一段回答里用一两个自然的习语就够了。2. **语境要对**：*It's raining cats and dogs* 这类过时或夸张的习语，母语者现在很少说，考官也不会加分。3. **不要背诵整段模板**：考官受过训练，能识别背诵的答案。
@end

## 1 · Buying time & organising ideas · 争取思考时间与组织表达

- that's a tricky question | phr. | 这是个不好回答的问题 | — | That's a tricky question. I've never really thought about it before. | S
- let me think | phr. | 让我想想 | — | Let me think… I'd probably choose my grandmother. | S
- off the top of my head | phr. | 不假思索地；凭印象 | — | Off the top of my head, I'd say about twenty. | S
- I'd say | phr. | 我会说；我觉得 | — | I'd say the biggest difference is the cost of living. | S
- I reckon | phr. | 我估计；我认为（英式口语） | — | I reckon most people would agree. | S
- the thing is | phr. | 问题是；关键是 | — | I'd love to travel more. The thing is, flights are so expensive. | S
- what I mean is | phr. | 我的意思是 | — | What I mean is, it depends on the person. | S
- to be fair | phr. | 说句公道话 | — | The food was a bit cold, but to be fair, the restaurant was very busy. | S
- having said that | phr. | 话虽如此 | — | I love living in Shenzhen. Having said that, it's very expensive. | S
  note: 口语中自然的让步转折，比 *however* 更口语化。同义：*that said*、*mind you*（英式）。
- mind you | phr. | 不过；话说回来（英式口语） | — | It's a great phone. Mind you, it wasn't cheap. | S
- at the end of the day | phr. | 归根结底；最终 | — | At the end of the day, it's your decision. | S
- more often than not | phr. | 往往；多半 | — | More often than not, I take the metro to school. | S
- every now and then | phr. | 偶尔；时不时 | — | I go hiking every now and then. | S
- once in a blue moon | phr. | 千载难逢；极少 | — | I only eat fast food once in a blue moon. | S
- back in the day | phr. | 以前；过去（怀旧语气） | — | Back in the day, my parents didn't have mobile phones. | S
- it brings back memories | phr. | 勾起回忆 | bring back memories of | That song brings back memories of primary school. | S
- it depends | phr. | 看情况 | It depends on… | It depends on how much time I have. | S

@cmp actually · in fact · really
actually | 其实（纠正误解、表示意外） | I thought it would be boring, but it was actually great.
in fact | 事实上（补充更强的信息，或纠正） | He's not lazy. In fact, he works harder than anyone.
really | 真的；非常（强调） | I really enjoyed it.
note: 中国学生口语中常过度使用 *actually*，几乎每句话都以它开头。它的核心作用是“与预期相反”，没有这层意思时不要用。
@end

@box ielts 口语 Part 2 示范（注意自然表达的密度）
*I'd like to talk about my grandmother. We've always **got on like a house on fire**. **Back in the day**, she ran a small noodle shop, and she still **goes the extra mile** for everyone in the family. **To be fair**, she can be a bit stubborn, but **at the end of the day**, she's the person I look up to most.*
——约 60 个词中只有 4 个习语，而且每个都用得自然。
@end
