## 2 · Artificial intelligence · 人工智能

* artificial intelligence | n. | 人工智能（AI） | the development of artificial intelligence | Artificial intelligence is changing many industries, from medicine to education. | W
  fam: artificial (adj.) · artificially (adv.)
  note: *artificial* = 人造的（≠ natural）：*artificial sweeteners, artificial light*。注意 AI 话题的写作要避免两个极端：既不要说“AI 将取代所有人”，也不要说“AI 毫无影响”。更有说服力的写法是区分哪些任务容易被自动化（重复、规则清楚），哪些不容易（需要判断、同理心、创造力）。
- machine learning | n. | 机器学习 | — | Machine learning allows computers to improve by learning from data. | W
- automation | n. | 自动化 | — | Automation has replaced many factory jobs. | W
  fam: automate (v.) · automated (adj.) · automatic (adj.)
- robot | n. | 机器人 | — | Robots now assemble most cars.
  fam: robotics (n.) · robotic (adj.)
- chatbot | n. | 聊天机器人 | — | Many companies use chatbots to answer customer questions.
- generative AI | n. | 生成式人工智能 | — | Generative AI can produce text, images and music. | W
- processing power | n. | 处理能力；算力 | — | Modern phones have more processing power than early supercomputers.
- facial recognition | n. | 人脸识别 | facial recognition technology | Facial recognition is used to unlock phones and pay for goods.
- deepfake | n. | 深度伪造（视频、音频） | — | Deepfakes make it harder to know what is real online.
- replace | v. | 取代；替换 | replace A with B; be replaced by | Will robots replace human workers?
  fam: replacement (n.)
  note: 结构：*replace A **with** B*（用 B 替换 A）；*A is replaced **by** B*（A 被 B 取代）。
- job displacement | n. | 工作岗位被取代 | — | Governments need policies to deal with job displacement caused by AI. | W
- human oversight | n. | 人工监督 | — | Important decisions made by AI should be subject to human oversight. | W
- transparency | n. | 透明度 | lack of transparency | People want more transparency about how algorithms make decisions. | W
  fam: transparent (adj.)
- accountable | adj. | 负有责任的；应负责的 | be held accountable for sth | Who should be held accountable when a self-driving car crashes? | W
  fam: accountability (n.)
- self-driving | adj. | 自动驾驶的 | a self-driving car | Self-driving taxis are already being tested in some cities.
  note: 也称 *autonomous vehicles*（自动驾驶汽车）、*driverless cars*。

## 4 · Technology & society · 科技与社会

- digital divide | n. | 数字鸿沟 | bridge the digital divide | The digital divide means poorer families have less access to technology. | W
  note: *digital divide* 指不同人群（城乡、贫富、年龄）在获取和使用数字技术上的差距。写作中可与 *access to technology*、*digital literacy* 搭配讨论。
- reliance | n. | 依赖 | reliance on technology | Our growing reliance on technology makes us vulnerable to cyberattacks. | W
- attention span | n. | 注意力持续时间 | a short attention span | Some people believe social media is shortening our attention spans.
  note: 常见说法“人类的注意力持续时间比金鱼还短（8 秒）”**没有可靠研究依据**，是被广泛传播的错误数据。引用时要小心——这本身就是一个关于 *misinformation* 的好例子。
- revolutionise | v. | 彻底改变；使发生革命性变化 | revolutionise the way we… | The smartphone revolutionised the way we communicate. | W
- disrupt | v. | 扰乱；颠覆（行业） | disrupt an industry; disrupt sleep | Ride-hailing apps disrupted the taxi industry. | W
  fam: disruption (n.) · disruptive (adj.)
- streamline | v. | 使（流程）简化、高效 | streamline a process | Online forms have streamlined the application process. | W
- keep pace with | phr. | 跟上……的步伐 | keep pace with change | Laws have not kept pace with technological change. | W
- double-edged sword | phr. | 双刃剑 | — | Technology is a double-edged sword: it brings both benefits and risks. | S W
- unplug | v. | 拔掉插头；（比喻）暂时远离电子设备 | — | It's good to unplug from technology for a few hours a day. | S

@cmp technique · technology · technical
technique | 技巧；方法（做事的具体方式） | She uses a special breathing technique before exams.
technology | 技术；科技（工具与知识系统） | Medical technology has improved rapidly.
technical | 技术上的；专业的 | We had some technical problems with the projector.
@end

@box ielts 写作：AI 会取代人类工作吗？
可用论点：*automate routine tasks*（自动化重复性工作）· *create new types of jobs* · *job displacement in manufacturing and customer service* · *the need for retraining and lifelong learning* · *tasks requiring empathy and creativity are harder to automate*。用 *may / is likely to* 等表达预测的不确定性（第四册 Unit 45）。
@end
